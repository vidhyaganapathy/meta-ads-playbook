# 02 · Creative testing

## Test in this order

| Round | Variable | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Concept / angle** | Problem-led vs result-led vs social proof |
| 2 | **Hook** | Same body, 3 different first 2 seconds |
| 3 | **Format** | Reel vs static vs carousel |
| 4 | **Creator vs brand** | Creator-led (see whitelisting-playbook) vs brand creative |
| 5 | **Copy and CTA** | Headline, primary text, button |

Change **one variable per round.**

## What makes a Meta ad work

- **Hook in the first 2 seconds.** Show the problem or the payoff immediately.
- **Product on screen early.** Don't make people wait to see what it is.
- **Captions on.** Most people watch muted.
- **Native look.** Creator-style and lo-fi often beat polished brand video.
- **One message per ad.** One problem, one benefit, one CTA.
- **Vertical 9:16 for Reels and Stories**, 4:5 for feed, with text inside the safe zones.

## Kill and scale rules

| Signal | Action |
|---|---|
| Spend reaches [2–3×] target CPL with no leads | Pause |
| CTR well below account average after enough impressions | Pause, retest hook |
| Beats target cost per qualified lead for [7] days | Scale in steps |
| Frequency rising, CTR falling | Refresh creative |

## Creative fatigue

Watch frequency and CTR together. Rising frequency with falling CTR means the audience has seen it enough. Refresh with a new hook on a proven body before rebuilding from scratch.
