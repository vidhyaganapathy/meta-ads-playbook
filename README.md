## Principles

- **Simple structures learn faster.** Fewer ad sets with more budget beat many small ones.
- **Creative is the targeting.** With broad audiences, the creative decides who responds.
- **Optimise for the event you actually want.** Qualified leads or activated users, not just clicks.
- **Kill rules before launch.** Every test has a spend cap and a number that ends it.
- **Agents read, humans decide.** Automation summarises; people change budgets.
