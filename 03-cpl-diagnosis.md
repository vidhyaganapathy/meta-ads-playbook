# 03 · CPL diagnosis tree

When cost per lead goes up, work down the funnel **in this order**. Fix the first thing that's broken before touching anything else.

```
CPL up?
│
├── 1. Tracking ─────── Are leads actually being recorded?
│                        Check: form/pixel/CAPI firing, leads in CRM match Ads Manager
│
├── 2. Audience fit ─── Is the ad reaching the right people?
│                        Check: audience changes, new exclusions, placement mix shifts
│
├── 3. Creative/hook ── Are people stopping and clicking?
│                        Check: CTR trend, hook rate, frequency (fatigue)
│
├── 4. Offer ────────── Is the offer still compelling?
│                        Check: competitor offers, seasonality, offer fatigue
│
├── 5. Landing page ─── Do clicks turn into leads?
│                        Check: page conversion rate, load speed, message match with the ad
│
└── 6. Lead form ────── Is the form adding friction?
                         Check: number of fields, qualifying questions, mobile experience
```

## Quick reference

| Symptom | Most likely cause | First fix |
|---|---|---|
| CTR dropped, frequency up | Creative fatigue | New hook on proven body |
| CTR fine, conversion rate dropped | Landing page or offer | Check page, message match |
| Leads in Ads Manager, not in CRM | Tracking / integration | Fix the connection first |
| Lots of leads, few qualify | Form too easy, audience too broad | Add a qualifying question |
| Sudden spike after an edit | Learning phase reset | Wait before judging |

## Rule

**Don't judge on one bad day.** Look at a full week, and compare cost per *qualified* lead, not just CPL.
